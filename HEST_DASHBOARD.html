Introduction

This project analyses student records from eight programs to see how attendance, study habits, scores, funding and wellbeing relate to academic success.

Scope

Covers enrolment cohorts, program and department performance, study mode, scholarships, first-generation status, risk levels and final outcomes (enrolled, graduated, at risk, withdrawn).

Outcome

A filter-driven dashboard that reacts to every selection, compares programs side by side, explores relationships between any two measures and documents each data-cleaning step.
